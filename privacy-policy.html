Residuo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 July 2026 · Last updated: 5 October 2026

Residuo is a personal finance app for iOS. It is built so that your financial records stay on your device and in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot read them.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Residuo is developed and distributed by an independent individual developer established in the European Union (“the developer”, “we”), who is the controller within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”).

Contact for all privacy matters: n.kalpashchykau@gmail.com. The developer’s registered name and contact details are also shown by Apple on the app’s App Store product page.

Because processing is limited and does not involve large-scale or systematic monitoring, no Data Protection Officer has been appointed (Art. 37 GDPR).

2. Scope

This policy covers the Residuo iOS app, its widget, and this web page. It does not cover Apple’s own services (iCloud, Sign in with Apple, the App Store, TestFlight), which Apple provides under its own Privacy Policy.

3. What the app processes, and on what legal basis

3.1 Data that stays with you

Everything you enter in the app - transactions, goals, monthly payments, income sources, categories and quick actions - is stored in a database on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, mirrored to the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account.

The developer has no access to this data and does not receive it. There is no application server, no account on the developer’s side and no backup outside your Apple Account. This processing happens locally, on your device and under your control, for the sole purpose of providing the app’s functionality (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR - performance of the contract you enter into by using the app).

3.2 Sign in with Apple

Signing in returns an Apple user identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email address. These values are written to the device Keychain and are used solely to keep you signed in and to link your data to your Apple Account. They are not transmitted to the developer. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.3 Exchange rates

If you record amounts in more than one currency, the app requests public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). Only currency codes are sent. As with any internet request, that provider receives your IP address and can infer approximate location from it; the developer does not receive or store it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - the legitimate interest in converting amounts into a single currency. You can avoid this request entirely by keeping a single currency in Profile → Currencies.

3.4 Diagnostics

Apple may share crash reports and performance statistics with the developer through App Store Connect and TestFlight, but only if you have enabled sharing in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Those reports are aggregated by Apple and contain no financial records. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - the legitimate interest in fixing defects. You can withdraw this at any time in iOS Settings.

3.5 What the app never does

Residuo contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no user profiling. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it makes no automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR).

5. International transfers

Data stored in iCloud may be processed by Apple on servers outside the European Economic Area. Apple applies the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and other safeguards under Chapter V GDPR; see Apple’s Privacy Policy for details. Exchange rate requests may likewise reach servers outside the EEA. The developer performs no transfers of its own, since it holds none of your data.

6. Retention

Your records are kept for as long as you keep them. They live on your device and in your iCloud account, and you delete them yourself: individual entries in the app, the whole history under Profile → “Erase history”, and everything at once - on this device and in iCloud, across all your devices - under Profile → “Delete account”. Signing out removes only the copy on the device. The developer stores nothing and therefore erases nothing on your behalf.

7. Your rights

Under Articles 15-21 GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. In practice you exercise most of these directly in the app and in iOS Settings, because you hold the data yourself and the developer has no copy to disclose, correct or delete.

For anything you cannot resolve in the app, write to n.kalpashchykau@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR).

Providing data is not a statutory or contractual requirement; the app simply cannot perform its function without the entries you choose to make.

8. Security

Access to the app is protected by a PIN and, at your option, Face ID or Touch ID. The PIN is stored as a salted hash in the device Keychain; biometric data is handled by iOS and is never available to the app. Data on the device is protected by iOS Data Protection, and the iCloud copy by Apple’s encryption. No method of storage is completely secure, and no absolute guarantee of security can be given.

9. Children

Residuo is not directed to children. It is not intended for use by anyone under 16, and the developer does not knowingly process the data of such users. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact the address above.

10. Users in California

The developer does not collect personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and does not sell or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. No financial incentives are offered, and no consumer is discriminated against for exercising their rights.

11. This web page

This page sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party content. Standard server access logs may be kept by the hosting provider.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes. Continued use of the app after a change indicates that you have read the updated policy.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: n.kalpashchykau@gmail.com. Requests are answered within one month, as required by Art. 12(3) GDPR.
